About the Summit

A journey of knowledge, reflection, and love for the Seerah.

The BRACUian Seerah Summit 1448 is a semester-long initiative by the students of BRACU, dedicated to exploring the blessed life, character, teachings, and timeless legacy of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ through authentic Seerah literature, meaningful reflection, and intellectual engagement.

Spanning multiple weeks, the Summit combines knowledge, competition, reflection, and collective learning through a series of engaging programs, culminating in a grand Seerah gathering featuring prominent scholars and Da'is from across the country.

Beyond competition, the Summit seeks to nurture the qualities exemplified by the Prophet ﷺ — tawheed, morality, leadership, mercy, honesty, discipline, patience, and empathy — while inspiring students to engage deeply with the Seerah and carry its timeless guidance into their everyday lives.
