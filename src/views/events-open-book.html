About this competition

The Seerah Open Book Examination is designed to move participants beyond factual recall and encourage deeper reflection, analysis, and application of lessons from the Seerah.

Format

Participants will receive a series of scenario-based questions, with multiple sub-questions incorporated within each scenario.

The examination will require participants to:

  • Study the relevant Seerah material
  • Analyse each scenario carefully
  • Answer all accompanying questions
  • Develop their responses in written form
  • Submit their completed write-up within the specified deadline

The open-book format is intentionally designed to encourage understanding and thoughtful engagement rather than memorisation alone.

Submission

Participants will be required to submit their completed written responses within the announced submission period.

Further instructions regarding the question format, submission process, evaluation criteria, and guidelines will be communicated to registered participants.
