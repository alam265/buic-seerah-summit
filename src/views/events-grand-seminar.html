About this event

The Summit will culminate in a Grand Seerah Seminar, bringing together the BRACU community for an immersive gathering centred around the life and legacy of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

The program will feature discussions and sessions on the Seerah delivered by prominent scholars and Da’is from across Bangladesh.

The Grand Seminar will serve as the culminating gathering of BRACUian Seerah Summit 1448, bringing together the learning, engagement, and reflections developed throughout the Summit.

The Grand Seminar will feature

  • Prominent Scholars & Da’is — Meaningful discussions on the Seerah and its contemporary relevance.
  • Seerah-Centred Sessions — Exploring the life, character, leadership, and legacy of the Prophet ﷺ.
  • Community Gathering — Bringing together students, alumni, scholars, and members of the wider BRACU community.
  • Summit Recognition — Recognition of outstanding participants and winners of the Summit’s competitive programs.
