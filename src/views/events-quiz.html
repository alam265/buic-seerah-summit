About this event

There will be a total of 6 quizzes. Every Friday, a new question set will be released on the Wayground website. Participants must submit the quiz by the following week.

Details with a video preview will be shared on our WhatsApp group by the start of the quiz.

Reference book

উসওয়াতুন হাসানাহ — the brief blessed biography of Rasulullah ﷺ

The Seerah of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is among the greatest subjects to study. Through it we learn how Allah prepared His Messenger, how a small community prevailed against great opposition, and how the beauty of Islam was raised amid falsehood — drawing on the Qur’an, authentic tafsir, hadith, and reliable Seerah sources.

Quizzes are based on this book. Collect your copy to prepare for each Friday quiz.
